**The most effective systems will rely on the strengths of humans and AI**. Humans bring critical thinking, judgment, creativity, and ethical oversight that AI may struggle to replicate. AI others speed, scale, pattern recognition, and ability to process vast amounts of information.

## **Capabilities**

**Models can shift between tasks without additional training**. AI models can write poetry, brainstorm ideas, help understand quantum computing, analyze quarterly business trends.

**Models can maintain the thread of a conversation**. They can remember what you discussed earlier, build upon it. Example: if you mentioned your project deadline and refer back to it later within the conversation, the AI typically understands what you're talking about.

**Many LLMs can reach beyond their knowledge**. They can use external tools and information sources. For example, they can search the web, process files, or even use other applications.

## **Limitations**

**Knowledge cutoff date**. AI models are bounded by their training data. LLMs have a knowledge cutoff date based on when they were trained, the point after which they have no innate knowledge of the world. For example, a model with cutoff date of November 2024 means that it wasn't trained on any date after November 2024.

**Incorrect information**. Models can learn and sometimes reproduce inaccuracies that were present in their training data. That happens because the training process doesn't verify every fact in the training data.

**Models can hallucinate**. They can confidently state something that sounds plausible but is actually incorrect.

**AI context window**. Every LLM has a limit to how much information it can consider during a single interaction. If this limit is exceeded, the AI won't be able to remember information that falls outside the window.

**Non-deterministic outputs**. LLMs are somewhat unpredictable. Ask the same question twice and you might get slightly different responses each time. Some LLMs offer settings that allow to control that randomness. This setting is often referred to as temperature.

**Limitations with complex reasoning tasks**. LLMs might struggle with logical or mathematical problems requiring multiple steps. The good news is that newer models get better in reasoning step by step.

**Lack of access to data**. If a model doesn't have access to a piece of data or tool that is needed to answer a question, then it shouldn't come as a surprise that it won't be able to answer the question.
