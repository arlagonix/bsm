## **A closer look at delegation**

**The delegation is about what work needs to be done, what work you should do yourself, and what work might be better suited to AI**. Delegation will help you work effectively and efficiently with AI assistants.

Delegation involves three key elements:

-  **Problem awareness**: Understand the problem you're trying to solve

-  **Platform awareness**: Know the strengths and limitations of AI systems

-  **Task delegation**: Divide work between yourself and AI.

## **Problem awareness**

**If you don't understand your goals, work needed to be done, you will struggle to effectively work with AI**. The most effective AI collaborators are experts in their fields first, and AI delegators second.

What you need to do:

-  Understand what you're trying to solve

-  Understand the capabilities of available AI tools

-  Break down the complex work into smaller parts

-  Define who does what

Answer the questions before involving any AI tools:

-  What does "success" look like?

-  What kind of thinking and work is needed to get there?

## **Platform awareness**

**You should know about available AI systems, about their capabilities and limitations**. Experiment as often as you can with different AI systems and develop your own insights.

-  Understand the strengths and limitations of available options.

-  Which models perform best for the work you have in mind?

-  Which systems prioritize speed, or creativity, depth, or accuracy?

## **Task delegation**

**Divide work between yourself and AI**.

Ask yourself questions:

-  What can you automate with AI?

-  Where would augmentation create more value than working separately?

-  What should you do alone without AI?

-  What can an agent do?
